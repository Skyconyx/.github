# Skyconyx Brand Assets

Current brand direction: **Beacon**.

The mark represents clarity cutting through operational complexity — a directional form built from connected translucent planes.

## Assets

- `skyconyx-beacon.svg` — primary standalone mark with transparent background
- `skyconyx-beacon-dark.svg` — dark app/icon treatment
- `skyconyx-beacon-light.svg` — light app/icon treatment

## Brand hierarchy

- Personal identity: **Skyieng**
- Business identity: **Skyconyx**
- Current offer: **Reconciliation Automation**

Do not create a separate product brand for Reconciliation until there is stronger market evidence that it deserves one.

## Visual direction

- Deep teal / green
- Charcoal
- Off-white
- Calm, precise, operational
- Avoid generic AI imagery, neon gradients, robots, brains and network clichés

The mark should remain recognizable at small sizes and work across GitHub, website UI, favicons and business material.
