### What we're building

Skyconyx focuses on operational problems that businesses are still paying people to repeat every day, week or month.

Our current offer is **Reconciliation Automation** — helping teams compare recurring Excel/CSV exports, identify matches and surface exceptions such as missing, mismatched, duplicate and unknown transactions.

**Current demo:** [demo.skyconyx.com](https://demo.skyconyx.com)

### How we think

- **Outcome before technology** — the business problem comes first.
- **Automation before replacement** — remove repetitive work while keeping human review where it matters.
- **Evidence before complexity** — validate demand before building heavy systems.
- **Clear exceptions, not more dashboards** — surface what needs attention and hide what does not.
- **Simple enough to deploy, useful enough to pay for.**
